# Angels and Demons
> BLG-02 · The Ivy Wolf Life · Mind, Body & Spirit · commissioned art · hero img-8166-2 (the light frame) · credit "The Wolf Life"
> **FIRST PASS — from the painting itself. The real meaning is yours to give; this is a starting draft for your feedback, not a finished story.**

Two faces, close enough to be one.

This is the first of the Mind, Body & Spirit paintings, and it is the one I keep coming back to. Not the loud one — the light one. Where the others go deep and cold, this one stays warm: creams and soft golds and a brown that reads more like earth than like shadow. The dark is still there. It has to be. But here it's the ground the light stands on, not the thing that wins.

I painted two faces because that's the truer picture of a person. Everyone carries both — the part that lifts and the part that pulls, the angel and the demon, and most days they aren't at war so much as leaning on each other. That's what I wanted: not good fighting evil, but the two of them holding still together long enough to look like peace.

Mind, Body & Spirit is the collection where I let the work get abstract — where a painting stops being *of* a place and starts being *of* a feeling. This one is about the moment the two halves of a person stop arguing.

— *Ivy Wolf*
