## collection_statement_en

TRANSMUTACIÓN — 2025

Expressionism with physical textures. Unique, unrepeatable works. Various formats.

Three lines of work:

1. Commissioned Art with Vision Session — Each piece is born from a deep conversation
   with the client about emotions, history and purpose, guaranteeing a unique and
   unrepeatable piece.

2. Independent Artistic Work — Pieces created on the artist's own initiative,
   unique and exclusive, available to collectors and design spaces.

3. Contemporary Decorative Paintings — Works made with acrylic paint, allowing fast
   drying and less production time without sacrificing quality or artistic identity.
   Free, expressive, versatile — connects directly with the modern viewer.

We do not sell decoration. We offer art with identity.

What the eyes see, the soul remembers.
— Ivy Wolf
