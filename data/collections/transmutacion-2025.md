## collection_description_en

TRANSMUTACIÓN is Ivy Wolf's current body of work — an expressionist series built in
physical texture and high relief. Each piece in this collection is an original,
unrepeatable work created from acrylic layered until the surface holds weight and emotion.

The series explores transformation: of material, of feeling, of the spaces that hold us.
Works range from intimate small formats to grand-scale paintings for significant spaces.

All works in TRANSMUTACIÓN are available for acquisition. Contact us to view available pieces.
