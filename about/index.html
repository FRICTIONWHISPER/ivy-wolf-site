About

Ivy Wolf

Portrait pending — PHASE-5.0

21 years of practice

"What the eyes see, the soul remembers."

Ivy Wolf is an expressionist painter whose work lives at the edge of memory and place. Each canvas is painted once — singular, unrepeatable — and carries a certificate of authenticity that seals this fact in the physical object itself.

Her practice spans commissioned works made for a specific person or company, and independent collection pieces painted on her own initiative. Both are originals. Only commissioned works are never reproduced in any form.

Ivy's approach to a commission begins with a Vision Session — a free conversation about the emotion, the space, and the story behind the work. The buyer provides the initial image. Ivy provides the rest.

Authenticity

The certificate of authenticity

Every original work leaves Ivy's studio with a signed certificate of authenticity. The canvas carries her signature on the back, sealed in UV resin. Each work holds a series number, a poetic text written for it alone, and a watermark. A commissioned work will never be reproduced in any form.

Collection pieces — painted on Ivy's own initiative — may be issued as a finite, numbered edition clearly labelled as such. The original stays singular and keeps its provenance.
